Academic Roadmap

The Four-Week Beginner Curriculum

Each week focuses on a core pillar of trading literacy, ensuring concepts are thoroughly understood before advancing to higher complexity.

Module 01 Week 1

Forex Fundamentals

Establish a solid theoretical understanding of foreign exchange markets, currency pricing, and transactional mechanics.

  • What is Forex? Global decentralized currency markets, daily turnover, and liquidity providers.
  • Currency pairs: Base and quote currencies, Majors, Minors, and Exotics.
  • Market sessions: London, New York, Tokyo, and Sydney operating hours and overlap volatility.
  • Pips, spreads, lots, margin and leverage: Exact mathematical definitions, contract sizes, and margin calculations.
Outcome: Fluency in financial currency terminology and quoting mechanics.
Module 02 Week 2

Market Analysis

Learn how to interpret market structure, read candlestick price action, and identify high-probability zones.

  • Candlestick charts: Anatomy of bullish/bearish candles, wicks, bodies, and key price rejections.
  • Trend analysis: Identifying Higher Highs, Higher Lows, Lower Highs, and trend breaks.
  • Support and resistance: Plotting horizontal key levels, flip zones, and multi-timeframe confirmation.
  • Basic technical analysis: Moving averages, trendlines, and objective chart markup standards.
Outcome: Ability to analyze any clean price chart without indicators clutter.
Module 03 Week 3

Risk Management

Master the protective framework that separates educated market participants from unguided speculators.

  • Risk-to-reward concepts: Designing trade setups with favorable asymmetry (minimum 1:2 or 1:3).
  • Position sizing: Calculating exact lot sizes based on 1% maximum portfolio risk per idea.
  • Drawdown: Preserving mental and financial capital through consecutive loss protocols.
  • Trading psychology: Overcoming fear, greed, FOMO, over-trading, and revenge executions.
  • Capital preservation: The non-negotiable rule of survival in speculative markets.
Outcome: Complete mastery of mathematically sound risk formulas.
Module 04 Week 4

Trading Platform & Practical Learning

Hands-on execution on professional terminal software, trade recording, and comprehensive course evaluation.

  • MetaTrader 5 introduction: Interface layout, chart configuration, and shortcuts.
  • Demo account setup: Connecting virtual funds through regulated broker demo servers.
  • Understanding order types: Market Execution, Buy/Sell Limits, Buy/Sell Stops, Stop-Loss, and Take-Profit.
  • Trading journal: Documenting entries, rationale, risk metrics, and post-trade analytical reviews.
  • Final assessment: Comprehensive examination of risk rules, chart analysis, and platform competency.
Outcome: Practical confidence operating MT5 with disciplined demo execution.
