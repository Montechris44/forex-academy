Privacy Notice & Data Policy

Last Updated: November 2026. This policy outlines how FOREX ACADEMY collects, protects, and handles applicant and student information.

1. Institutional Overview & Scope

Forex Academy is dedicated to delivering professional financial education. We respect the confidentiality and privacy of every prospective student and participant. This Privacy Notice governs the handling of information submitted through our application forms, admissions desk inquiries, and communications channels.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential personal information required to review cohort applications and organize training schedules:

  • Personal Contact Information: Full legal name, email address, WhatsApp/phone number, and state/city of residence.
  • Academic Profile: Prior trading experience, learning objectives, preferred device, training delivery format (online or physical), and preferred schedule.
  • Verification: Confirmation that you are at least 18 years of age.

3. Purpose of Processing

Your information is collected strictly for legitimate educational operations:

  • Verifying student eligibility and reviewing cohort application availability.
  • Transmitting onboarding packages, lecture access links, and MetaTrader 5 setup guides.
  • Communicating schedule adjustments or class announcements via email or WhatsApp.
  • Issuing formal certificates of completion following final course assessments.

4. Data Storage & External Service Providers

The Forex Academy website is a static educational platform. We do not maintain local databases, nor do we store student information in browser localStorage, cookies, or tracking scripts.

When you complete an application, your submission is routed directly to our designated secure external form service (such as Tally.so or Google Forms) and our admissions desk. These third-party services maintain industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption.

5. No Selling or Commercial Sharing

Forex Academy maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against the commercialization of student data. We will never sell, lease, monetize, or distribute your email address, phone number, or details to third-party brokers, marketing networks, or signal providers.

6. Retention and Deletion

Application records are retained solely for the duration necessary to administer the training cohort and fulfill basic educational audit standards. You may request the permanent deletion or rectification of your contact information at any time by contacting our Admissions Desk.

7. Contact Admissions

For privacy inquiries, data modification requests, or questions regarding our instructional policies, please reach our administrative office:

  • Email: admissions@forexacademy.edu
  • WhatsApp Desk: +234 800 000 0000
